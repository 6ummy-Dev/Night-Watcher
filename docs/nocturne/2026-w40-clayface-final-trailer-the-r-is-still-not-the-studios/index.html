Clayface has a final trailer. The R is still not the studio's.

The Board

  • DatedDynamic Duo

The week came in with the names first. The pages that should have held the dates did not all agree.

01screenReported

Warner Bros. posted a trailer it calls final.

Warner Bros. uploaded "Clayface | Final Trailer" on 24 September. The line under it says only in theaters October 23. The official site says North America on October 23, 2026, and internationally from 21 October. WarnerBros.com still reads Only in Theaters October 23.

The one-sheet carries the same date. No rating is printed on it.

The Numbers lists an MPA rating of R, bulletin 2954, certificate 56141, dated 16 September: strong bloody violence, gore, language, some nudity and brief drug use. Fandango still shows the film as not rated. filmratings.com returned no Clayface row.

The date is the studio's. The letter is still a listing. The paperwork always burns last.

02comicsConfirmedOff the map

Williamson named Batman: Justice Year. DC's site has not.

Joshua Williamson put the title on his own account. DC's pages do not.

On 29 September and 1 October he posted pictures and tagged DC and Batman. Neither caption names a title.

On 2 October he wrote "Batman: Justice Year", then "From me and @Bruno_Redondo_F in 2027" then "More info next week..." A second post the same afternoon calls the picture a teaser and credits Bruno Redondo and @fxstudiocolor_. That is the title, and that is the year. It is not a month, a format, or an issue count.

ComicsBeat names colourist Adriano Lucas and says the book will launch next year. AIPT says the release date and the format have yet to be revealed. DC's home page, the New York Comic Con page, and a shop search still return no title string.

The name is his. The calendar is not.

03screenReported

Four voices for Dynamic Duo, and no Batman among them, TheWrap says.

Deadline reports that Dynamic Duo moves from 30 June 2028 to 22 September 2028. It calls the film a Robins-origin feature, puppets meet animation.

TheWrap confirms four voices. Mason Thames as Dick Grayson. Logan Kim as Jason Todd. Lee Pace as Red Hood One. Steve Buscemi as Red Hood Two.

Deadline has both dates. The four names are TheWrap's. A Batman voice is not among them.

04comicsReportedOff the map

Absolute Batman stops after #25

CBR says Absolute Batman goes on hiatus after #25, solicited for 28 October. It calls that issue oversized, at 30 pages, and says the book returns in 2027 with a guest artist on #26. Comic Book Club says the December solicits show no Absolute Batman, and names a Beyond Ark M Special. It does not give that special a cover date or an on-sale date.

Bleeding Cool relays Scott Snyder on a podcast. It does not give #26 or #27 a cover date or an on-sale date. That is the street.

The gap is in the solicits. A month after it is not.

05toysConfirmedOff the map

Hot Toys dates Absolute Batman for Japan only.

Hot Toys Japan dates the sixth-scale figure for January 2027. Both pages call it a first production run, and both say the sale is Japan only. The general-sale page lists a height of about 41cm and twenty points of articulation.

CM#030 is general sale. CM#030B is limited to Toysapience, and adds a cape with a shawl.

No street date outside Japan is on these pages.

06otherConfirmed

Late wires

Penguin Random House lists Batman by Neal Adams: Absolute Edition 1967-1970 as published on 29 September 2026, 408 pages.

DC announced the Absolute Batman Artist Collection. The shop has the jacket, the battle-axe pin, and the Dragotta tote.

Warner Bros. posted a trailer for Batman Knightfall Part 2: Knightquest on 19 September. The description says coming soon. The title page still has no street date.

Blokees lists a Batman: Hush Champion Class kit.

The city stays wet. Clayface does not. The file's open again next Sunday.
