03The Sunday machine

  1. Mon → Sun noonThe news window. A sweep every morning Monday to Saturday and a last one before Sunday noon: the street files leads, the wire opens pages and files cards, and what is worth keeping goes in the notebook.
  2. Sun 12:38 → 17:00The desk writes the issue: the Night Editor’s draft, the SEO pass, the copy desk, pictures, the catalogue stamp last, then the build and the check. The Night Editor writes the one X draft into the pull request.
  3. Sun by 17:00One pull request, opened as nocturne-night-final[bot]. The fence lets it touch four paths: nocturne/issues/, docs/nocturne/, nocturne/NOTEBOOK.md and docs/sitemap.xml, which guard 167 holds to the paper’s block.
  4. Sun 17:00–21:00The owner reviews and merges. By the owner’s rule, not a ruleset, nothing else goes to main while it is open.
  5. On mergeCloudflare deploys; the front, the feed, the sitemap and the card follow on their own.
  6. Sun 22:00–23:00The live page is checked: a 200 and the merged headline. Then the owner posts the Night Editor’s draft on X. No desk posts.
  7. Not merged by 23:00The issue does not run. Still-news carries to next Sunday.

Times are Montevideo. A thin week is fine; a padded one is not. No news, no issue.

04The research loop

  1. DeliverAn outside team delivers its files.
  2. DigClaude reopens every cited page and tries to disprove each claim that would change the repo.
  3. SortEach claim is held, improved, unconfirmed or wrong, with a brief for the next round.
  4. RuleThe owner rules. Only then does anything go into a release.

The harness runs on every push and pull request: the guards, smoke, the negative wall (every guard made to fail on purpose before it is trusted), and a real browser, Chromium and WebKit, with axe. Paper-only pull requests run every guard and skip smoke and the wall; every push to main runs all of it.

Claude’s QA reports read the whole repo, each finding with a repro. Independent auditors read the live release. The owner’s eye covers what no harness sees: real devices, VoiceOver, High Contrast, the Sunday review.

A QA-driven cut takes every finding in one release, and anything left out gets its reason. Standing decisions do not reopen because a scanner proposes their opposite.

08The rules

  1. The owner merges. No bot, no session, no auditor lands anything.
  2. Every fact has a source that was opened. Unopened is unconfirmed.
  3. Plans and mocks before code. Measured, not eyeballed.
  4. One source of truth per thing: one rating source per show, one owner per rule file, one voice per issue.
  5. No new third-party services. Use what is already paid for.
  6. Short, direct, transparent. Show the method; say what was not checked.
