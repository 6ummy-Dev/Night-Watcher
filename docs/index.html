Every Batman story ever filmed

Night Watcher lists 133 films and 67 seasons of television across 44 continuities, in Batman watch orders that spoil nothing: by universe, by the arc of one life, or by release. Next up names the next unwatched entry, and Progress keeps the tally. The app needs JavaScript; what follows is what it covers.

The eras of Bruce’s life

  1. Before Batman — Gotham before there is anyone to answer it. Alfred, the Waynes, and a boy.
  2. The early years — Established and still alone. No Robin yet — just a man and a corrupt city.
  3. The Grayson years — A partner in the car. The lightest stretch of the whole life.
  4. Rebuilding — A harder, lonelier Batman. Dick has moved on to Nightwing and a later Robin is in the car — usually Tim Drake, with Barbara around.
  5. Broken and rebuilt — The years he is taken off the board, and comes back anyway.
  6. The League years — Batman as one of a team — a shared headquarters, joint missions — and still a partner in the car.
  7. No one in the car — The League is an institution now — a roster of dozens — and he works it alone. No partner beside him, which is the one thing every entry here shares.
  8. The Damian years — A son raised by assassins, learning the rule.
  9. The last years — Retired, and back out anyway.
  10. Beyond — An old man in a chair, and a kid in a new suit.
  11. After the cowl — Gotham without him. Three series that each start after he is out of the picture, and are about the city that is left.

Straight answers

Which order should I watch Batman in?

Three honest answers instead of one fake one. By universe: Every universe kept whole, each in its own story order, and the universes themselves in the order their stories start. Bruce’s life: One life, in order — from before there is a Batman to after there is not. Release order: Straight release order, 1943–2028.

Does this include the animated series?

Yes — all of it. 67 seasons of television ride alongside the 133 films, animated and live action both, and a switch narrows the shelf whenever you want it narrower.

Do I have to watch everything?

No. 74 titles are marked as the essentials and the core route — the spine of the thing. Everything else is optional side material, labeled as exactly that.

Is it really spoiler-safe?

That is the whole premise. Universes stay whole, and every ordering is built so nothing renders ahead of an entry it would give away.

Where can I watch all of these?

Availability changes constantly and differs by country, so nothing here stores an answer that would rot — every entry carries a fresh where-to-watch search instead.

Does it track what I watch?

No. There is no account and no sign-in, no advertising, and nothing tracking what you watch. What you tick stays in your browser and is never sent anywhere. It works offline, and it is free software under the AGPL.

Where do the Joker films fit?

In their own continuity, and nowhere near the rest. They are Gotham stories with no Batman in them, so they sit in a universe of their own, marked optional — nothing else in the catalogue depends on having seen them.
