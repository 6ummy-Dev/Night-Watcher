## Clayface has a final trailer. The R is still not the studio's.

Warner Bros. posted a trailer it calls final. The date is on the studio's own pages. The rating is not.

[Warner Bros.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KCR-rz0YfD4) uploaded "Clayface | Final Trailer" on 24 September. The line under it says only in theaters October 23. [The official site](https://www.clayfacemovie.com/trailer/) says North America on October 23, 2026, and internationally from 21 October. [WarnerBros.com](https://www.warnerbros.com/movies/clayface) still reads Only in Theaters October 23. Nothing on those pages describes the footage, and this paper will not either.

The [one-sheet](https://www.clayfacemovie.com/images/share.jpg) the movie site shares carries Only in Theaters October 23. No rating is on it. It is not a frame from the trailer.

[The Numbers](https://www.the-numbers.com/movie/Clayface-%282026%29) lists an MPA rating of R, bulletin 2954, certificate 56141, dated 16 September: strong bloody violence, gore, language, some nudity and brief drug use. [Fandango](https://www.fandango.com/clayface-2026-244768/movie-overview) still shows the film as not rated. A search on [filmratings.com](https://www.filmratings.com/Search?filmTitle=Clayface) returned no Clayface row this Sunday.

The date is the studio's. The letter is still a listing.

## Williamson named Batman: Justice Year. DC's site has not.

Joshua Williamson put the title on his own account. DC's pages still do not print it.

The pictures with this story are earlier, and they do not name the book. On [29 September](https://x.com/Williamson_Josh/status/2104994620853846501) and [1 October](https://x.com/Williamson_Josh/status/2105745190199636171) he posted them and tagged DC and Batman. The posts have no title. The 29 September picture prints "INVOLVING THE JUSTICE LEAGUE IS A RISK." The 1 October picture prints "GET OUT OF MY CITY. NOW!" Both credit Joshua Williamson and Bruno Redondo. Neither prints the title.

On 2 October he wrote ["Batman: Justice Year"](https://x.com/Williamson_Josh/status/2106062906408227135), then "From me and @Bruno_Redondo_F in 2027," then "More info next week..." A [second post](https://x.com/Williamson_Josh/status/2106081787730616364) the same afternoon calls the picture a teaser and credits Bruno Redondo and the colour account he tagged. That is the title, and that is the year. It is not a month, a format, or an issue count.

[ComicsBeat](https://www.comicsbeat.com/batman-justice-year-williamson-redondo-dc-comics/) names colourist Adriano Lucas and says the book will launch next year. That is a trade. It is not a DC solicit. [AIPT](https://aiptcomics.com/2026/10/02/williamson-redondo-batman-justice-year/) says the release date and the format have yet to be revealed. [DC's home page](https://www.dc.com/), the [New York Comic Con page](https://www.dc.com/nycc), and a [shop search](https://shop.dc.com/search?q=Justice%20Year) still return no title string.

The name is his. The calendar is not.

## Deadline moves Dynamic Duo to 22 September 2028. TheWrap names four.

[Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/09/dynamic-duo-gremlins-release-dates-1237111978/) reports that Dynamic Duo moves from 30 June 2028 to 22 September 2028. The piece calls it a Robins-origin feature.

[TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/movies/dynamic-duo-cast-mason-thames-lee-pace-steve-buscemi/) confirms four voices. Mason Thames as Dick Grayson. Logan Kim as Jason Todd. Lee Pace as Red Hood One. Steve Buscemi as Red Hood Two.

The date is a trade date. The four names are TheWrap's. A Batman voice is not among them.

## Absolute Batman goes quiet after issue 25

[CBR](https://www.cbr.com/absolute-batman-hiatus-date-confirmed/) says Absolute Batman goes on hiatus after the 28 October release of issue 25, per the solicits. It calls that issue oversized, at 30 pages, and says the book returns in 2027 with a guest artist on issue 26. [Comic Book Club](https://comicbookclublive.com/2026/09/20/absolute-batman-seemingly-going-on-hiatus-comic-book-industry-doomed/) says the December solicits show no Absolute Batman, and names a Beyond Ark M Special. It does not give that special a cover date or an on-sale date.

[Bleeding Cool](https://bleedingcool.com/comics/what-comes-after-absolute-batman-25-and-why-the-wait-until-2027/) relays Scott Snyder on a podcast. It does not give issue 26 or issue 27 a cover date or an on-sale date. That is the street.

The gap is in the solicits. A month after it is not.

## Hot Toys Japan dates Absolute Batman for January 2027

Hot Toys Japan put a date on the sixth-scale figure. The pages say the sale is Japan only.

[CM#030](https://www.hottoys.jp/item/view/4570191994403.php) is general sale, scheduled for a January 2027 release. [CM#030B](https://www.hottoys.jp/item/view/4895228624253.php) is the same month, limited to Toysapience, and adds a cape with a shawl. Both pages say for sale in Japan only.

No street date outside Japan is on these pages.
