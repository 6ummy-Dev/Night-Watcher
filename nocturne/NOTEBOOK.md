# The notebook

What the desk has read, filed one fact at a time. The desk keeps this file:
it reads every day, writes here what it learned, and files the week's
entries in its own pull request, normally Sunday's issue. It is never
published. It is where the stories come from, and where "From the morgue"
comes from.

The rules (`BRIEF.md` §2, the notebook):

- Under a week heading, `## 2026-W40`, oldest week first.
- One fact per line, dated the day it was read, linked to where it was read:
  `- 2026-09-28 — The fact, in one sentence. [Where](https://…)`
- A fact needs a source the way a story does. No drafts, no loose notes,
  nothing from a leak.
- A line from the page or the screen is welcome: one quotation per entry, 25
  words at most, exact, with its work in italics.
  `- 2026-09-28 — Batman, in *Batman: Year One* #1 (1987): “…” [Where](https://…)`
- `npm run nocturne:check` refuses anything else.
